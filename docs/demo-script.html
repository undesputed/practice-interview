Before you start

  • ☐Test camera and microphone 10 minutes early.
  • ☐Confirm the deep-learning emotion model is on (EMOTION_ANALYSIS=1) — without it, the "Emotion (deep model)" card won't appear.
  • ☐Close other tabs and apps — stop pop-ups and noise.
  • ☐Turn off notifications on your computer.
  • ☐Sit facing good, even light — the face tracking is the star of this demo.
  • ☐Know the fallback: an old saved session under History.
  • ☐Practice this script out loud once, with a clock.

How to say the names

  • molave.ai → moh-LAH-veh dot A-I
  • Claude → rhymes with "loud"
  • MediaPipe → two plain words: "media" + "pipe"
  • FACS → sounds just like the word "fax"

Live face tracking — MediaPipe

2:30 – 4:00

Now, let me show you the smart part of this tool.

Open the Face Analysis screen, or point to the live camera view

pause

The camera uses a technology called MediaPipe. Say it like two words: "media" and "pipe."

MediaPipe finds points on my face. It finds about fifty points, in real time.

pause

It also finds my hands and my body posture — all at the same time.

All of this happens inside the browser. My video never leaves my computer.

pause

From these face points, the tool reads my facial muscles.

This uses a method called FACS. Say it like the word "fax."

pause

Scientists use FACS to study small face muscles — like a lowered brow, or a tight lip.

From these muscles, the AI guesses my emotion, many times every second.

Smile, or show surprise, to trigger a reaction effect

Watch. If I smile, the tool sees "happy." Small sparkles appear near my face.

pause

This happens live, as I talk. It is not a video filter — it is read from real muscle movement.

Say — "The camera needs a clear, front-facing view. Let me try again." Face the camera directly and repeat the expression.

If something goes wrong

  • Camera or mic will not connect: refresh the page once. If it still fails, say — "Let me show you a finished report from an earlier session instead," and open History.
  • Internet is slow or the AI does not answer: wait 5 seconds, then use the "thinking" line from Scene 03. If it still fails after 10 seconds, click "End Interview" and move straight to the Report.
  • The "Emotion (deep model)" card is missing: this means EMOTION_ANALYSIS was not turned on for this session. Skip that line and continue — "Emotional tone" and "Facial signals" (MediaPipe + FACS) still work regardless.
  • You lose your place: the rail on the left matches the app screens in order. Find your current screen, find that section, and continue from there.
