00WHY

Interview prep is mostly guesswork.

You can rehearse your answers, but no one measures your eye contact, your posture, your speaking pace, or your filler words. Feedback is vague and subjective — if you get any at all.

molave.ai turns all of that into numbers — and into coaching you can act on.

01WHAT IT IS

One session. Three kinds of feedback.

RUNS IN THE BROWSER

No install, no upload

Computer vision and a live voice agent run straight in the browser — nothing to set up.

PRIVACY-FIRST

Your video never leaves you

Only numeric measurements are sent to the server. The video itself stays on your device.

ONE READINESS SCORE

Voice + presence + answers

Three signals are fused into a single 0–100 readiness score with a plain verdict.

02HOW A SESSION WORKS

Set up → interview → report

STEP 01

Set up

Pick the role, focus (behavioral / technical / mixed), difficulty, and the interviewer's tone — friendly to intimidating.

STEP 02

Interview live

An AI interviewer asks questions out loud while the camera quietly reads your on-screen presence.

STEP 03

Get your report

A readiness verdict, per-pillar scores, detailed metrics, charts, and written coaching.

03CORE FEATURE

The live interview

A Deepgram Voice Agent runs the whole conversation over a WebSocket. Claude Sonnet 4.6 is the “think” model that decides what to ask; Aura-2 speaks it aloud. Choose the interviewer's tone and each maps to a different voice.

THINK

claude-sonnet-4-6

Reasons about your answers and asks the next question in real time.

SPEAK

aura-2 TTS

Four voices — friendly, professional, stern, intimidating — set by your chosen tone.

TRANSPORT

WebSocket

Low-latency, two-way audio streaming secured by ephemeral tokens.

07INSIDE THE THREE PILLARS

What each pillar measures

40% · DELIVERY

Voice

From Deepgram timings + browser acoustics
  • Pace 110–160 wpm
  • Fillers < 3 / 100 words
  • Long pauses ≤ 2
  • Pitch variation ≥ 25 Hz
  • Energy steady loudness
35% · PRESENCE

Face & body

Composites from MediaPipe landmarks
  • Attention gaze + steadiness
  • Confidence upright + steady body
  • Composure steady head + body
  • Calm 100 − nervousness
25% · CONTENT

Answers

Judged by Claude from the transcript
  • Clarity — is the point easy to follow?
  • Structure — beginning, middle, end
  • Specificity — real examples, not fluff
  • Relevance — does it answer the question?
08NOT A BLACK BOX

Every presence score is an explicit formula

These run over signals derived from face and pose landmarks — transparent, testable, and covered by the test suite.

attention = 0.5·gaze + 0.3·steadiness + 0.2·face-presence
confidence = 0.5·upright + 0.5·body-steadiness
composure = (head-steadiness + body-steadiness) / 2
nervousness = 0.25·blink + 0.25·(100−gaze) + 0.2·face-touch + 0.15·fidget + 0.15·tension
Gaze from eye blendshapes · posture from shoulder/nose geometry · tension from FACS action units (AU4 + AU7 + AU23).
09FACS · ACTION UNITS

The face, read muscle by muscle

FACS — the Facial Action Coding System — is the standard way to break any expression into individual muscle movements called Action Units. MediaPipe's blendshapes act as ARKit-style proxies for these units, and the app maps them to 17 Action Units.

10ENGINEERING CHOICES

Choices that respect the user

01

In-browser vision

MediaPipe runs on-device; video stays with the user.

02

Audio is discarded

Recorded audio is analyzed for delivery, then thrown away — never stored.

03

Ephemeral tokens

Short-lived Deepgram tokens keep API keys out of the browser.

04

Reweight, not penalize

Absent signals redistribute weight, so partial results stay fair.

05

No build step

Vanilla JS + FastAPI — simple to run, test, and deploy (Docker / EC2 / ECS).

06

Tested rubric

The scoring logic is covered by a pytest suite — scores are reproducible.
